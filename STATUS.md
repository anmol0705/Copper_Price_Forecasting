- [x] Track C (general, non-finance GNN structure-learning-collapse literature) complete subagent: sonnet-DD model: Sonnet.
  MAJOR FINDING: our exact architecture (softmax(ReLU(E1@E2^T)) bilinear adjacency) is NOT a novel design — it's the "self-adaptive adjacency matrix" from Graph WaveNet (2019) and MTGNN (2020), the same MTGNN already cited as a baseline in our own paper. A 2026 paper ("Diagonal Adaptive Graph," Info journal, doi.org/10.3390/info17040394) directly stress-tested this EXACT mechanism family (GWNet/MTGNN/D2STGNN/BigST) and found: under identical training but different random seeds, the learned adjacency varies substantially while predictive performance stays nearly unchanged — i.e. the graph structure is UNDERDETERMINED by the forecasting objective, a general property of this mechanism, not a bug specific to our codebase. This is strong independent, third-party, peer-reviewed-adjacent confirmation of our own diagnosis. Their own fix was to ABANDON free-form bilinear adjacency learning and constrain the parameterization (diagonal-restricted) rather than trying to regularize it into working.
  3 candidate fixes ranked by the research agent (none validated at our exact small-N financial scale — genuine literature gap, not glossed over):
  1. HIGHEST PRIORITY: Gumbel-softmax/discrete edge sampling (NRI/GTS-style) — mechanistically different from our 2 failed attempts because it changes the LOSS LANDSCAPE SHAPE (hard categorical draw per step = no easy "spread evenly" solution available) rather than adding a penalty on top of an unchanged landscape that still has uniform as an easy basin. NRI validated at N=5 (comparably small to our N=8) in a physics setting with real recovered structure — the closest small-N precedent found.
  2. SECOND: contrastive/self-supervised auxiliary loss on the structure itself (SUBLIME-style) — most principled since it manufactures a second gradient source independent of the weak downstream task signal, but unvalidated at N=8, adds real design complexity (what does "graph augmentation" even mean for 8 financial variables).
  3. THIRD, WITH STRONG CAVEAT: sparsity/entropy regularization directly opposing uniformity — cheapest, textbook-precedented (Cini et al., JMLR 2023, explicitly names "collapse to uniform" as a known failure mode) but genuinely risky given OUR OWN finding that the task is close to indifferent to structure at N=8: could manufacture a confident-LOOKING but arbitrary graph instead of fixing anything, indistinguishable from real structure without independent validation. Recommends a seed-stability check as the tell (per the DiAG finding) — if the recovered adjacency changes materially across random seeds while loss stays flat, that itself falsifies "meaningful structure" regardless of how non-uniform it looks.
  Also ruled out/deprioritized: temperature annealing alone (controls sharpness, not WHICH structure to sharpen toward — no gradient signal means no benefit without also doing #1), curriculum/two-stage pretraining (no literature found actually validating this for graph STRUCTURE specifically, weakest-evidenced idea), oversmoothing/PairNorm/DropEdge fixes (confirmed mechanistically DIFFERENT problem — layer-wise inference-time smoothing vs. our training-time parameter collapse — correctly already not pursued).
  Research agent's own honest bottom line: if Gumbel-softmax AND contrastive auxiliary loss BOTH fail to move the needle at N=8, that itself would be informative — would suggest 8 variables genuinely carry too little independent structural information for this class of method, and the DiAG paper's own response (stop learning free-form adjacency, constrain the parameterization instead) may be the more defensible path than continuing to chase regularizers.
